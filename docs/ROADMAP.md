# Roadmap

The steps of Kete Team. Each closes on a proof: a test that names it, and what was seen in the
demonstration. « Built » is not « proven with a real company »: nothing here has been used by one
yet.

| Step | Branch      | What it gives                                                                                     | Proof                                                       | State |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| H1   | `001-team`  | The roster, authorisations that expire, what a project requires, assignments, leave, weekly time  | « A person without a valid authorisation cannot be assigned » | Built |
| H2   | `002-reach` | A reminder before an authorisation expires and a leave decided by a reply, on WhatsApp and Telegram | « An expiring clearance is said to its holder on her phone »  | Next  |
| H3   | `003-cost`  | The cost of a day by person, and each project's labour cost told to the center                    | « A project's labour cost follows the weeks' answers »      | Later |

## What waits

- Advances on salary: they are money, asked here and paid with Kete Finance's advances.
- Public holidays and the balance of annual leave.
- A demonstration joined to Kete Enterprise's: the center's projects and its leave circuit, for
  real.
- A deployment, and a pipeline: the repository is private and the organization's Actions are
  refused for billing; the checks are run on the workshop's machine.
